# Job intake — for Craig

The town and service pages have a "Recent work" section that stays hidden until
there is at least one real job in `content/projects.yml`. This is how a two-minute
voice note becomes a case study without you writing anything.

**How it works:** pick a job from the last 12 months you're happy to talk about,
answer the six questions below into a voice note (one job per note is fine, or
rattle off a few). Send them to Matt. Matt transcribes and pastes them in.

Nothing gets published that you haven't said. No location, customer type, result
or quote is ever invented.

## The six questions

1. **Where was it?** Suburb, and was it a house, business, farm, school or unit block.
2. **What were they worried about?** In their words if you remember — break-in,
   staff theft, kids home alone, can't see the front gate, no internet at the shed,
   whatever it was.
3. **What did you put in?** Brand, how many cameras or sensors, and anything unusual
   you had to solve — long cable run, heritage house, salt air, no power at the gate.
4. **What changed for them afterwards?** Anything they said, or anything you noticed
   on a follow-up visit.
5. **Did they say anything you'd be happy to quote,** and can we use their first name?
   If not, that's fine — we'll describe the job without a name.
6. **Got a photo?** Even a phone shot of the finished install or the NVR screen.
   Drop photos in `assets/projects/` (Matt will resize them).

## Two optional questions that help the pricing and FAQ sections

- What does a typical 4-camera home CCTV install cost, roughly, and what pushes it up?
  (Right now the FAQ says "contact us for a quote" because we don't have a range.)
- Do you do interconnected photoelectric smoke alarms for the 1 January 2027 QLD
  deadline? And what's your QLD security provider licence number (for the record —
